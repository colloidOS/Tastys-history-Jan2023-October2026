# Changelog

All notable changes to the Tastys prototype.

## 0.7.1 - August 2024

- Likes and comment counts on feed posts.

## 0.7.0 - July 2024

- Activity feed on Home (FeedCardView).

## 0.6.2 - June 2024

- Xcode 15.4. Leaderboard tab with top dishes and medals.

## 0.6.1 - May 2024

- Dietary filters on Search (Vegan, Halal, Keto, ...).

## 0.6.0 - April 2024

- Plates: save dishes to your plate (PlateStore, YourPlateView).

## 0.5.2 - March 2024

- Rate a dish: Fire / Chef's Kiss / Solid / Meh reactions.

## 0.5.1 - February 2024

- HomeViewModel: loading/error states, list logic moved out of views.

## 0.5.0 - January 2024

- APIClient protocol with MockAPIClient, preparing for a real backend.

- LocationManager. Restaurants show distance and sort by nearest.

## 0.4.0 - October 2023

- Moved to Xcode 15 / iOS 17. Placeholder Profile tab.

## 0.3.2 - September 2023

- Bookmarks: FavoritesStore persisted in UserDefaults.

## 0.3.1 - August 2023

## 0.3.0 - July 2023

- Search screen with text filter and cuisine chips (Category Icons).

## 0.2.2 - June 2023
